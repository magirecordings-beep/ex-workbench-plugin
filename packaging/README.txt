EX Workbench (plugin) - plays E-MU Emulator X banks (.exb + SamplePool) in Ableton and other hosts.

INSTALL
1. Right-click "Install.command" and choose Open (macOS asks because it was downloaded). It copies the AU and VST3
   into your plug-in folders and the standalone app into ~/Applications.
2. In Ableton: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan. EX Workbench appears under Plug-Ins (by "EX Workbench").

USE
- Click "Load bank..." and choose the .exb. Its SamplePool folder must be next to it (as on the X3 disk).
- Ableton saves the bank location, preset, knobs and settings with your project.
- Program changes pick presets (bank select CC32/CC0 for blocks of 128). Knobs A-P listen to X3's default CCs
  (21-28, 70-73, 91, 93, 82, 83); click a knob's tag to MIDI-learn it. All knobs can be automated in Ableton.
