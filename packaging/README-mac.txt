Xtreme Lab (plugin) - plays E-MU Emulator X banks (.exb + SamplePool) in Ableton, Logic and other hosts.

INSTALL
1. Right-click "Install.command" and choose Open (macOS asks because it was downloaded). It copies the AU and VST3
   into your plug-in folders and the standalone app into ~/Applications.
2. In Ableton: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan. Xtreme Lab appears under Plug-Ins (by "Xtreme Lab").

USE
- Click "Load bank..." and choose the .exb. Its SamplePool is found automatically: a "SamplePool" or
  "<bank name> SamplePool" folder next to the .exb, or .ebl files lying beside it.
- Your project saves the bank location, preset, knobs and settings.
- Program changes pick presets (bank select CC32/CC0 for blocks of 128). Knobs A-P listen to X3's default CCs
  (21-28, 70-73, 91, 93, 82, 83); click a knob's tag to MIDI-learn it. Everything can be automated.
- Filter: Cutoff shift and Resonance move every voice's filter of the preset; centre = as the preset.
